Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 jan. 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how users of the mnsx website (the "Site"), a personal music portfolio, are informed about the collection, use and protection of their personal data.

1. Data Controller

The data controller is:

  • Contact: mnsxcontact@gmail.com
  • Website: music.mnsx.org

2. Personal data collected

The Site may collect the following personal data:

  • First and/or last name (via contact form)
  • Email address (via contact form)
  • Message content (via the contact form)
  • Browsing data (IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent), for statistical purposes

No personal data is collected without the user's knowledge.

3. Purposes of processing

Collected personal data is used only for the following purposes:

  • Responding to messages sent via the contact form
  • Enabling professional or artistic exchanges
  • Improving the user experience and the Site's content
  • Producing anonymized visitor statistics

4. Legal basis for processing

The processing of personal data is based on:

  • The user's consent (contact form, cookies)
  • The legitimate interest of the data controller to ensure the proper operation and security of the Site

5. Cookies

The Site may use cookies or similar technologies to:

  • Measure the Site's audience and performance
  • Improve navigation

The user can configure their browser to refuse some or all cookies. Refusing cookies may limit certain Site features.

6. Data retention

Personal data is retained only for the time necessary for the purposes for which it was collected:

  • Contact data: up to 3 years after the last exchange
  • Browsing data: according to the legal retention period in force or anonymized

7. Data sharing

Personal data is not sold, exchanged, or rented to third parties.

Data may, however, be hosted or processed by technical service providers (site host, analytics tool), solely for the operation of the Site and in compliance with GDPR.

8. Data security

The data controller implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure the security and confidentiality of personal data.

9. User rights

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users have the following rights:

  • Right of access
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure (right to be forgotten)
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to object
  • Right to data portability

To exercise these rights, users may contact the data controller at: mnsxcontact@gmail.com.

10. Complaints

If a user believes their rights have not been respected, they may lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority (in France: CNIL – www.cnil.fr).

11. Changes to this privacy policy

This privacy policy may be updated at any time to remain compliant with legal, regulatory or technical changes. The update date will be indicated at the top of the document.
