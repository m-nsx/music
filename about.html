EXPERIMENTAL MUSIC

Music has always been a space for exploration and intuition. What began as curiosity—experimenting with sounds, textures, and software—quickly turned into a deep fascination with electronic music as a creative process. Working between sound creation and experimentation, I found an interest in shaping atmospheres and creating evocative soundscapes.

Over time, my practice has shifted towards ambient and experimental genres, where textures, nuances and immersion prevail. Inspired by electronic music, abstract soundscapes, and audiovisual art, I explore music as an immersive experience.

If you would like to make a suggestion, ask a question, or simply chat, please feel free to contact me.
